Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains how the NicheSpark Chrome extension (“the extension”) and the website nichespark.tech (“the website”) handle information. In short: from version 0.4.0, you sign in to use the extension and your research is saved to your NicheSpark account so it is backed up and available on any computer. Nothing else about your browsing is collected.

Versions before 0.4.0 have no accounts: they keep all research data in your browser only, as described under “What the extension stores”, and send it nowhere.

Your account

To use NicheSpark 0.4.0 or later you create a free account, either with an email address and password or with Google.

  • Email sign-up: we store your email address, the name you enter, and your password as a secure one-way hash. We never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Google sign-in: we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We do not get access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • We also keep sign-in sessions (with the IP address and browser that started them) to keep you signed in and protect your account.

Account and research data are stored with our database provider Neon (in the United States). Sign-in requests pass through a small service on nichespark.tech hosted by Netlify, which does not store them.

Research data in your account

While you are signed in, the projects, queries and keywords described below are copied to your account and kept in sync with every browser where you sign in. Only you can read them: every request is checked against your account, and other users cannot see your data. We do not look at your research except when you ask us for support, and we never sell it or use it for advertising.

What the extension stores

The extension stores the following in your Chrome profile (IndexedDB and chrome.storage.local), and from version 0.4.0 also in your account:

  • Projects you create: name, seed keyword, chosen Google domain and language, and prefix, suffix and template lists.
  • Generated queries and their status (pending, done, empty or failed), including error messages.
  • Keyword suggestions captured from Google Autocomplete, with the queries and methods that found them, timestamps and counts.
  • Collection progress and small interface preferences, such as the last opened project.

Your sign-in session is also kept in chrome.storage.local so you stay signed in. Signing out removes your research data and session from that browser; it stays in your account.

What the extension reads

  • The extension runs only on the supported Google Search domains listed in its manifest.
  • On those pages it only uses the search box and the autocomplete suggestions, and only while you are running a collection.
  • Your own search-history entries that Google shows in the dropdown are recognized and are not saved.
  • It does not read or collect your browsing history, cookies, passwords, form data or the content of other websites.

Communication with Google

To collect suggestions, the extension enters your generated queries into the Google search box in a browser tab, just as you would by typing. Google receives these queries as normal searches typed in your browser, and Google's own privacy policy applies to that interaction. NicheSpark does not add identifiers or send any other data to Google.

Permissions

  • storage and unlimitedStorage: to save your projects and keywords locally without running into browser storage limits.
  • Access to supported Google Search domains: to run the collection script on Google Search pages only.
  • identity: to open the Google sign-in window when you choose “Continue with Google”.
  • alarms: to sync your account in the background every few minutes.

Analytics, ads and selling data

The extension contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. We do not sell, rent or share your data, except with the service providers named above (Neon for the database and sign-in, Netlify for hosting), who process it only to run NicheSpark. The website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. Our hosting provider (Netlify) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, for security and operations.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual keywords or whole projects in the dashboard; deletions sync to your account and your other browsers. Removing the extension or clearing its site data deletes the copy in that browser only; your account keeps its copy. To delete your account and all of its data, message us from the contact below with the email address of the account and we will delete it within 30 days. You can export any project as CSV or JSON at any time.

Children

NicheSpark is a professional research tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date. Significant changes will also be noted in the project's release notes.
