About NicheSpark

NicheSpark is a free Chrome extension for keyword research. It collects the suggestions Google Autocomplete really shows, organizes them by project, and lets you filter, group and export them, so you can find the long-tail keywords people actually type.

Who builds it

NicheSpark is built by Ahmed Raza at Cybrum Solutions, an AI-native software company in Karachi, Pakistan. Cybrum Solutions also runs AskSEO, a knowledge base for its SEO courses.

Why it exists

Google Autocomplete is one of the best free sources of real search phrases, but collecting it by hand means typing a query, waiting for the dropdown and copying each suggestion, hundreds of times. NicheSpark does that loop for you at a respectful pace and keeps everything organized, so the time goes into choosing keywords instead of copying them.

How we work

  • Real data only. NicheSpark saves what Google displayed in your browser. It never invents keywords, search volumes or difficulty scores.
  • Respectful collection. One query at a time with a delay, always started by you. If Google asks for verification, NicheSpark stops and waits.
  • Your data stays yours. Projects sync only to your own account. They are never sold or used for advertising. See the privacy policy.
  • Built in the open with users. Every release is listed in the changelog, and the roadmap shows what is planned.
