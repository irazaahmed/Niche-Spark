Changelog

What's new in NicheSpark

Every release of the extension, newest first. Chrome installs updates automatically after the Chrome Web Store approves them.

  1. v0.4.1Latest 12 October 2026

    Email verification and a theme switch

    Released on the Chrome Web Store.

    • Added Email verification: new email accounts confirm their address with a 6-digit code sent from no-reply@nichespark.tech, with a "Send a new code" option.
    • Added Light and dark theme switch in the popup and dashboard. Your choice is remembered; until you choose, NicheSpark follows your system setting.
    • Fixed First-time sign-in with Google now returns to the extension instead of opening the NicheSpark website.
  2. v0.4.0 12 October 2026

    Accounts and cloud sync

    Released on the Chrome Web Store.

    • Added Free NicheSpark accounts: sign up with email and password or continue with Google. Signing in is required to use the extension.
    • Added Cloud sync: projects, queries and keywords are saved to your account and kept in sync on every computer where you sign in. Collection still works offline; changes upload when you are back online.
    • Added Research you collected before 0.4.0 is uploaded to your account the first time you sign in.
    • Added Password reset by email, and a sync status with "sync now" next to your account.
    • Changed Signing out uploads any remaining changes, then removes your data from that browser; it stays in your account.
  3. v0.3.1 12 October 2026

    Dashboard controls fixed; Prefix, Suffix and Template ready out of the box

    Released together with 0.4.0.

    • Changed New projects start with ready-to-use prefix, suffix and template lists, so Prefix, Suffix and Template research work straight away.
    • Changed The popup offers one-click ready-made lists when a project's prefix, suffix or template list is empty.
    • Changed In the dashboard, Start is always available: if you've edited a list, "Save list & start" saves it and starts collecting in one click.
    • Fixed Start, Retry failed, Pause, Resume and Cancel in the dashboard now work. Before, they could fail with "The NicheSpark background worker did not respond" because commands from the dashboard tab were rejected.
  4. v0.3.0 12 October 2026

    Questions, ready-made lists and keyword grouping

    Released on the Chrome Web Store.

    • Added Questions research method: puts how, what, why, can, is and other question words before your seed, so Google suggests the questions people actually ask.
    • Added Ready-made prefix and suffix lists (Commercial, Budget, Pricing, Local, Learning, Business) that you add to a project in one click and can edit before saving.
    • Added Keyword grouping: every keyword gets a theme (Questions, Price & cost, Local, Compare & reviews, Business & jobs, Learning & guides), with a Theme filter and column.
    • Added Groups by modifier: one-click chips for words that recur after your seed, such as "course" or "prices", to filter the table.
    • Added CSV exports include a Theme column.
  5. v0.2.1 12 October 2026

    Cleaner A to Z results

    Released together with 0.3.0.

    • Added A Clean up button in the dashboard removes echo keywords saved by earlier versions, after asking you to confirm.
    • Changed An A to Z query where Google only echoed the query back is now marked Empty instead of Done.
    • Fixed A to Z research no longer saves Google echoing the unfinished query back, such as "mobile repairing a". Real completions like "mobile repairing app", and prefix or suffix keywords like "mobile repairing near me", are still saved.
  6. v0.2.0 12 October 2026

    Export, filters and the Chrome Web Store

    Released on the Chrome Web Store.

    • Added Search, filters by research method and source query, and six sort orders in the keyword table.
    • Added Copy all, filtered or selected keywords, one per line.
    • Added CSV export of all, filtered or selected keywords, UTF-8 so Urdu and other scripts open correctly in Excel.
    • Added Backup (JSON) for a whole project, and a validated import that always restores as a new project.
    • Added Delete selected keywords, with confirmation.
    • Changed New NicheSpark logo.
  7. v0.1.1 12 October 2026

    A fresh look

    Beta (manual install).

    • Changed New emerald colour scheme and typography in the popup and dashboard.
  8. v0.1.0 11 October 2026

    First beta

    First beta (manual install).

    • Added Live collection of real Google Autocomplete suggestions from a background Google tab.
    • Added A to Z, prefix, suffix and template research, with a query preview.
    • Added Pause, resume, cancel and retry failed, with progress saved after every query.
    • Added Automatic deduplication and source-query tracking for every keyword.
    • Added Stops for Google verification or consent pages and never bypasses them; your own search history is never saved.
