What is NicheSpark

Long-tail keyword research, without copy and paste

Google Autocomplete is one of the best free sources of real search phrases, but collecting it by hand means typing a query, waiting for the dropdown and copying each suggestion, hundreds of times. NicheSpark does that loop for you, at a respectful pace, and keeps everything organized by project.

Real suggestions only

NicheSpark reads the suggestions Google actually displays in your browser. It never invents keywords or shows sample data as results.

Organized automatically

Duplicates are merged (ignoring case and extra spaces), and every keyword remembers which queries and methods found it.

Private by design

No account, no server, no tracking. Your projects and keywords are stored only in your own Chrome profile.

    Research methods

    Four ways to expand any niche

    Queries are generated deterministically from your seed keyword. Accidental duplicates are removed, and invalid input is caught before collection starts.

    A to Z

    Adds every letter after your seed to uncover the full range of completions. You can start from any letter.

    • mobile car detailing a
    • mobile car detailing b
    • mobile car detailing c … z

    Prefix

    Puts your own words before the seed to find commercial and comparison intent.

    • best mobile car detailing
    • affordable mobile car detailing
    • professional mobile car detailing

    Suffix

    Adds words after the seed to find local, pricing and service queries.

    • mobile car detailing near me
    • mobile car detailing cost
    • mobile car detailing services

    Templates

    Build your own patterns with {seed} and {letter}. A template with a letter expands to 26 queries.

    • how much does {seed} cost
    • {seed} for {letter}
    • is {seed} worth it
    Features

    Built for reliable, honest research

    Pause, resume, cancel, retry

    Full control over every collection job. Retry only failed queries, and already completed queries are skipped automatically.

    Progress that survives

    Progress is saved after every query. Close the popup, come back later and continue exactly where you stopped.

    Respectful pacing

    A configurable delay of at least 2 seconds, with random variation, between queries. Only one job runs at a time.

    Honest status reporting

    Each query is marked done, empty or failed, with the reason. Failures are never counted as completed.

    Source tracking

    See every query and method that found a keyword, when it was first and last collected, and how often it appeared.

    18 Google domains

    Research google.com, .co.uk, .com.pk, .co.in, .de, .com.au and more, with your choice of interface language.

    Multiple projects

    Keep a separate keyword collection for every niche or client. Rename, reopen or delete projects any time.

    Your history stays out

    Your own past searches that Google mixes into the dropdown are recognized and never saved as keywords.

    Export, copy and back up

    CSV for spreadsheets, one-click copy for any tool, and a JSON backup you can restore as a new project.

    Filters and bulk actions

    Search, filter by method or source query, sort six ways, then copy, export or delete just the keywords you selected.

    FAQ

    Common questions

    Is NicheSpark free?

    Yes. NicheSpark is free and needs no account. Questions or feedback? Message us on WhatsApp.

    Where do the keywords come from?

    From the Google Autocomplete dropdown in your own browser. NicheSpark enters each query into the Google search box, waits for the suggestions to finish loading and saves what Google displayed. It does not generate or guess keywords.

    Does NicheSpark show search volume or keyword difficulty?

    No. NicheSpark collects autocomplete suggestions, which show what people search for, but it does not invent volume or difficulty numbers. You can take your list to the keyword metrics tool of your choice.

    Why might my results differ from someone else's?

    Google personalizes suggestions by location, language, sign-in state and trends. NicheSpark records exactly what Google showed your browser at that moment.

    Will Google block me?

    NicheSpark uses conservative delays and runs one query at a time, the way a person would. If Google shows a verification page anyway, the job stops and waits for you. It never tries to get around it. Please use NicheSpark responsibly and in line with Google's terms.

    Is my data uploaded anywhere?

    No. Everything is stored locally in your Chrome profile. NicheSpark has no server and sends your research nowhere. Removing the extension or clearing its data deletes your projects, so download a JSON backup from the dashboard regularly.

    How do I install and update it?

    Install it from the Chrome Web Store with one click. Chrome keeps it updated automatically.
